## 3. Conventions chosen (recorded in code headers; B02 documents them in the rules corpus)

- **D2 Hora**: Parashara — odd signs 0–15° Sun (Simha), 15–30° Moon (Karka); even signs reversed. **D30 Trimsamsa**: BPHS — odd signs Mars 5 / Saturn 5 / Jupiter 8 / Mercury 7 / Venus 5 → Mesha, Kumbha, Dhanu, Mithuna, Tula; even signs Venus 5 / Mercury 7 / Jupiter 8 / Saturn 5 / Mars 5 → Vrishabha, Kanya, Meena, Makara, Vrishchika. All 15 fork sign maps were checked against BPHS ch. 6 (header of `src/vargas/index.ts`). Degree inside a varga sign = (degree in D1 sign × N) mod 30.
- **Ashtakavarga**: BPHS bindu tables (fork); totals 48/49/39/54/56/52/39 = 337 asserted; house strength ≥ 28 / 25–27 / ≤ 24.
- **KP**: KP ayanamsa default (Settings may pass Lahiri/Raman), Placidus with the fork's semi-arc trisection and mean obliquity for the intermediate cusps (Lagna/MC with true obliquity); planets in Placidus houses by cusp span; significators A/B/C/D; node agents separate; `polar` at |lat| ≥ 66°.
- **Yogas**: whole-sign houses from the first house; Moon-chart mode evaluates Lagna rules from the Moon and flags `fromMoonChart`, house-lordship yogas are skipped; Parashara full aspects (Mars 4/8, Jupiter 5/9, Saturn 3/10); cancellations as explicit bhanga ids (§4).
- **Varshaphal**: solar return by bisection on the sidereal Sun (natal ayanamsa); `year` = Gregorian year of the return, `age = year − birth year`; annual chart at the birthplace unless a place is given; Muntha = natal Lagna + age signs (Moon sign in Moon-chart mode, `approx`); year lord = B.V. Raman's algorithm (Varshaphala ch. 4): candidates Muntha lord, natal Lagna lord, annual Lagna lord, tri-rashi lord (fire Sun/Jupiter, earth Venus/Moon, air Saturn/Mercury, water Mars/Mars by day/night), dina/ratri lord; only candidates aspecting the Lagna qualify, friendly aspect (3/11, 5/9) beats inimical (1/7, 4/10), then highest Panchavargiya bala, tie → dina/ratri lord; no aspect → strongest with `method: …-no-aspect-fallback`. Panchavargiya bala = Tajika Neelakanthi six-level values (kshetra 30/22.5/15/7.5/3.75/1.875, uccha 20 × (180 − d)/180, hadda 15…, drekkana 10…, navamsa 5…), compound friendship = Parashara natural × temporal (2,3,4,10,11,12), classes by Raman (< 5 weak, 5–10 ordinary, 10–15 powerful, ≥ 15 very strong); the worked example in Raman reproduces these unit values. Tajika aspects sign-based (no orbs). **Mudda dasha**: FEAT-B01 convention — Vimshottari proportions (years/120) over the exact year (this return → next return), starting from the natal Moon's nakshatra lord; the classical "nakshatra + age + 3" start was NOT used (binding FEAT text). Transit calendar: Sun, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu sign intervals.
- **Muhurat** (design 10.5 + N18): candidates = daytime choghadiya segments (+ night when `nightAllowed`) clipped by Rahu Kaal, merged on identical met-sets; the 10 rules in the order tithi, nakshatra, vara, chandra-bala, tara-bala, rahu-kaal, bhadra, panchak, abhijit, choghadiya; tithi/nakshatra/Moon sign of a window = the interval covering most of it; Bhadra rule fails only for earth-Bhadra; Chal choghadiya = neutral (rule not met); natal rules require every given profile to pass and read `not met / no-profile` without one; `partial` below 4 rules; ranking rules met → Abhijit → earlier start → longer. Activity tables follow Muhurta Chintamani / Muhurta Martanda / Dharmasindhu (sources in the JSON; the exact tithi/nakshatra/vara lists are the coder's reading of those texts — owner-reviewable).
- **Matching** (calibrated on the 20 Drik pairs, CONVENTIONS 1c): Vashya half-signs for Dhanu/Makara; Nadi exceptions same-rashi-different-nakshatra and same-nakshatra-different-pada; Bhakoot without the same-lord exception (named as a note only) and 0 for identical nakshatra-pada; Yoni = fork 4-level table (six documented differences); Mangal dosha houses 1,2,4,7,8,12 from Lagna/Moon/Venus with five cancellation rules + the both-manglik row; Drik truncates its printed total — the engine keeps exact half points ("23.5 of 36 guna").
- **Share code**: 2 + 17 + 11 + 2 + 15 + 16 + 10 + 2 + 8 + 17 bits; CRC-8 poly 0x07 over the 13 packed bytes with the CRC field zero; unknown time = 1440 minutes; lat/lon at 0.01°; tz = index into the sorted canonical zone list (344 zones < 1024); URL numbers never carry "+".
- **Eclipse rule** (Holika Dahan): umbral lunar eclipse whose maximum lies in the Purnima and whose umbral phase is above the horizon at the place → dahan on the eclipse day's pradosh after moksha (`pradosh-after-eclipse-moksha`); penumbral eclipses ignored. Delhi/Chennai 2026 now 03-03 / 03-04 as Drik prints; 2027/2028 unchanged.
- **Report models**: panchang at birth from the Vedic day of the instant; avakahada (varna, vashya, yoni, gana, nadi, charan, rashi, lagna, nakshatra lord, tatva, name syllable); Ghatak chakra from the Muhurta Chintamani ghata verses — the prahar column of some printed tables is omitted (no verse could be confirmed); "How this was computed" lines (ayanamsa + value, node method, ephemeris, tzdb release, house system, rounding, convention rule ids); disclaimer key `disclaimer.report`.
