2. **C-M2 (F9 copy)** `settings.exactAlarmsBody` says "Without it the change can be up to 2 minutes late", but on Android 12+ the OS stretches the 2-min `setWindow` to 10 min (C05 §4.4 `window=+10m0s0ms`; `WidgetScheduler.kt:22` comment).
