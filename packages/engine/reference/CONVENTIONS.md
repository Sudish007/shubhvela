# Reference conventions (spec section 6 validation suites)

What the engine computes, what the reference almanacs print, and every place where the two differ on
purpose. Written by the A04 validation step from the fetched reference data under this folder; the suites in
`test/suites/` encode these rules. Nothing here is a tolerance fudge: an entry either documents a convention the
engine adopted to match the reference, or an intentional difference with its classical basis.

## 1b. Festival rules adopted from the references (A04 recovery, 2026-2028 Delhi pages)

| Rule | Convention (engine after A04) | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Ekadashi fasts (every `*-ekadashi`, `gita-jayanti`, `dev-uthani-ekadashi`) | udaya day; two Ekadashi sunrises (vriddhi) → the later day; when the following Dwadashi touches no sunrise (kshaya) the fast stays on the day the Ekadashi began so the parana falls inside the Dwadashi (`ekadashiVrata` in the table; the vrat module's Smarta day follows the same two rules) | 72 Drik Ekadashi dates 2026-2028: 65 plain udaya, 2 vriddhi → later day (Padmini 2026, Vijaya 2027), 4 kshaya-Ekadashi begin days, 3 kshaya-Dwadashi begin days (Pausha Putrada 2027, Jaya 2028, Utpanna 2028); all 72 reproduced |
| Akshaya Tritiya | udaya with the tri-muhurta rule: the Tritiya must run ≥ 3 muhurtas (144 min) after sunrise, else the previous day (`minMuhurtas: 3`, Nirnaya Sindhu) | Drik 2026-04-19 (next-day Tritiya lasts 1 h 37 m), 2027-05-09 (3 h 28 m), 2028-04-27 |
| Vasant Panchami | purvahna-vyapini: Panchami at sunrise + ¼ daylight, first day when both | Drik 2028-01-31 (Panchami 07:15 → next day 09:28) |
| Sharad Purnima (Kojagara), Kalabhairav Jayanti | nishita (tithi at midnight) | Drik 2026-10-25, 2027-10-14, 2028-10-02; Kalabhairav 2027-11-20, 2028-11-09 |
| Maha Navami | the day before Vijaya Dashami (Navami joined with Ashtami is kept, Navami joined with Dashami rejected — Nirnaya Sindhu) | Drik 2026-10-19 / 2027-10-08 / 2028-09-26 while plain udaya gave the Dashami day in all three years |
| Narasimha Jayanti, Bhai Dooj, Radha Ashtami | when the tithi covers the reference kala (pradosh / aparahna / madhyahna) on two days, the later day | Drik 2026-04-30, 2026-11-11, 2027-09-08 |
| Vat Purnima | pradosh-vyapini Purnima | Drik 2028-06-06 (Purnima 12:30 → next day 11:38) |
| Vishu | Malayalam month start: the sankranti day only when the sankranti is before sunrise, else the next day (`dayRule: sunrise`); Puthandu keeps the sunset rule | Drik Vishu Kani 2026-04-15, 2027-04-15, 2028-04-14 vs sankrantis 04-14 09:40, 04-14 15:33, 04-13 21:48 |
| Baisakhi | the civil date of the Mesha sankranti (`dayRule: same-day`) | Drik 2028-04-13 (sankranti 21:48) |
| Onam, Thaipusam, Karthigai Deepam | the day whose DAYLIGHT the nakshatra covers most (a sunrise inside the nakshatra is not required) | Drik Onam 2027-09-12 (Thiruvonam 09-12 02:59 → 09-13 06:05, one minute after sunrise), Thaipusam 2027-01-22 (Pushya 10:25 → next day 07:31) |
| Krishna Janmashtami | Jayanti yoga: the Vedic day on which Ashtami and Rohini coincide at any instant is taken even when the Ashtami is at nishita only on another day; otherwise nishita, Rohini deciding between two days | Drik 2027-08-25 (Ashtami 08-24 20:25 → 08-25 19:20, Rohini from 08-25 12:28) |
| Festival list vs region | Drik's city pages list festivals of every region (Pongal, Onam, Vishu for Delhi); the suite evaluates both regions at the city and the app's region filter stays a display preference | 2026-2028 Delhi pages |
| Madhyahna festivals (Bhishma Ashtami, Rama Navami, Ganga Saptami, Ganesh Chaturthi, Rishi Panchami) | `prefer: 'vyapti'` (Phase A fixer, Chennai 2026-2028 pages): when the tithi holds the madhyahna reference instant on two days, the day on which it covers MORE of the madhyahna kala (sunrise + 0.4 .. 0.6 × daylight) is taken, ties → first day (Dharmasindhu adhika-vyapti) | Drik Bhishma Ashtami 2028: Delhi 02-03 (kala coverage 78.8 vs 78.0 min) but Chennai 02-04 (71 vs 95 min) — the Ashtami runs 02-03 12:21 → 02-04 12:47 and both madhyahna instants (Delhi 12:35, Chennai 12:22) fall inside it; all 5 madhyahna slugs × 3 years × 2 cities agree with Drik under the rule |
| Karthigai Deepam | `at: 'sunset'`: the day whose sunset falls inside Krittika (lamps at dusk); when no sunset is inside the nakshatra, the daylight-coverage rule above | Drik 2027: Chennai 12-11 (Krittika 17:38 → sunset 17:43) vs Delhi 12-12 (sunset 17:24 before Krittika); 2026 and 2028 unchanged for both cities |
| Lunar eclipse on a pradosh Purnima (Holika Dahan, `bhadraFree` + `pradosh`) | when an umbral (partial/total) lunar eclipse whose maximum falls inside the Purnima is visible at the place (Moon above the horizon during some umbral phase), the Purnima is eclipse-afflicted (grahana-sutak, Dharmasindhu): the dahan moves to the pradosh kala of the eclipse day after the moksha (last umbral contact), even when the Purnima has ended by then (the Pratipada evening). Penumbral-only eclipses carry no sutak. `convention: pradosh-after-eclipse-moksha`, `eclipse: {kind, start, peak, end, visible}` on the occurrence (B01, carry-over A-REVIEW-MEDIUM-1) | Drik Holika Dahan 2026-03-03 / Holi 2026-03-04 for Delhi and Chennai (total eclipse 15:19 -> 18:47 IST, Purnima 03-02 17:56 -> 03-03 17:08 under Bhadra until 05:28); 2027 and 2028 unchanged |

## 1c. Matching (Ashta Kuta) conventions adopted from Drik Panchang (B01, 20 reference pairs fetched 2026-10-27)

The engine's Moon longitudes agree with the longitudes Drik prints on its matching page to ≤ 0.01° on all 40 births, so every koota difference is a table convention, not an astronomy difference. `src/matching/index.ts` (`ashtakoota()`) follows the fork's Muhurta Chintamani tables with these calibrations:

| Koota | Convention (engine after B01) | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Vashya | Dhanu = Manava in its first half (0–15°), Chatushpada in its second; Makara = Chatushpada in its first half, Jalachara in its second; the fork had simplified both to one class | m04 (Meena–Dhanu 27°: Jalachara–Chatushpada = 1), m16 (Dhanu 1° Mula–Dhanu 24° P.Ashadha: Manava–Chatushpada = 1) |
| Nadi | same nadi = 0, lifted to 8 when the Moon signs are the same with different nakshatras (fork) OR the nakshatra is the same with different padas (new); the same nakshatra AND pada keeps the dosha | m02 (both Uttara Phalguni, padas 4/2 → 8), m15 (both Jyeshtha 3 → 0) |
| Bhakoot | 2/12, 5/9 and 6/8 = 0 with NO same-lord exception (Drik gives Mesha–Vrishchika 0); the traditional exception is only named (`bhakootNote`); both Moons in the same nakshatra and pada = 0 (eka-nakshatra eka-pada) | m17 (Mesha Bharani – Vrishchika Anuradha = 0), m15 (identical Moons = 0 although 1/1), m16 (both Dhanu, different nakshatras = 7) |
| Yoni | fork table kept: same 4, neutral 2, enemy 1 (three pairs), sworn enemy 0 (the seven classical pairs). Drik grades friendly pairs 3 and unfriendly pairs 1 from a 14 × 14 table B01 could not source (five pages consulted give the seven enmity pairs but no full matrix); the six pairs where Drik's table differs by one point are listed in section 3 as documented differences and the Yoni table is an open item for the Phase B fixer / B02 | m03, m06, m09, m19 (Horse with Sheep/Cow/Cat: Drik 3, engine 2), m08 (Horse–Serpent: Drik 1, engine 2), m13 (Sheep–Lion: Drik 1, engine 2) |
| Varna, Tara, Graha Maitri, Gana | fork tables unchanged | 20/20 pairs agree |
| Guna total | Drik prints the per-koota half points (Vashya 0.5, Tara 1.5, Maitri 0.5) but TRUNCATES the printed total to a whole number (its own kootas sum to 14.5 on m03 and 23.5 on m04 while it prints 14 and 23); the engine keeps the exact sum ("N of 36 guna" may read 23.5) and the suite compares `floor(engine total)` with Drik's total | m03, m04, m06, m07, m12, m17, m18, m19, m20 (every pair with a half point) |

## 3. Intentional festival-date and koota differences

Entries keyed `slug@city-year` are accepted by `festivals.suite.test.ts`, entries keyed `koota-<id>@<pair>` by `matching.suite.test.ts` (the total must then differ by exactly that koota); every other difference is a failure.

- `koota-yoni@m03`, `koota-yoni@m19` (Sheep–Horse: Drik 3, engine 2), `koota-yoni@m06` (Cow–Horse: Drik 3, engine 2), `koota-yoni@m09` (Cat–Horse: Drik 3, engine 2), `koota-yoni@m08` (Horse–Serpent: Drik 1, engine 2), `koota-yoni@m13` (Sheep–Lion: Drik 1, engine 2): Drik Panchang scores Yoni on a five-level table (same 4, friendly 3, neutral 2, unfriendly 1, sworn enemy 0); the engine keeps the fork's four-level table (no "friendly" grade, three "unfriendly" pairs) until the full 14 × 14 Yoni Kuta table Drik follows is sourced (section 1c). Open item for the Phase B fixer: obtain the table (Muhurta Chintamani Vivaha prakarana commentary / Drik's own Yoni page), replace `calculateYoni`, remove these six keys. Totals differ by exactly the Yoni point in each pair.

- Holika Dahan / Holi 2026 (Delhi and Chennai) were listed here through Phase A (engine 03-02 / 03-03 vs Drik 03-03 / 03-04, no eclipse model). B01 added the eclipse rule (section 1b), the engine now prints 03-03 / 03-04 and the keys were removed so a regression fails the suite again. 2027 (03-21 / 03-22) and 2028 (03-10 / 03-11) agree with Drik.
- `ganga-dussehra@delhi-2026`, `ganga-dussehra@chennai-2026`: engine 2026-06-24 (nija Jyeshtha Shukla Dashami; Adhik months carry no festival, Dharmasindhu). Drik lists 2026-05-25, the Adhik Jyeshtha Dashami. Kept as the Adhik rule; the same rule gives Drik's dates for Nirjala Ekadashi (06-25) and Vat Savitri (06-15) in the nija month.
- `sheetala-ashtami@delhi-2028`, `sheetala-ashtami@chennai-2028`: engine 2028-03-18 (Chaitra Krishna Ashtami at sunrise 06:27 Delhi / 06:14 Chennai, tithi until 17:30). Drik prints 03-17 for both cities, where the Ashtami begins at 16:26. Drik's rule for Basoda is still not inferable: its Sheetala Saptami is 2027-03-28 (Saptami 03-28 15:05 → 03-29 17:11, udaya would be 03-29) and 2028-03-16 (Saptami 03-16 16:12 → 03-17 16:26), so neither Saptami nor Ashtami follows udaya there, while 2026 and 2027 Ashtami agree with udaya; every Drik date is reproduced by "tithi at sunrise + 0.84–0.88 × daylight, first of two" (Phase A fixer, 2 cities × 3 years), but no classical source for such a kala is known, so it is recorded as unresolved, not as an engine change (carry-over A-NIT-1).
